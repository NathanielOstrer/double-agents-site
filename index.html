Double Agents is a word game for two players on the same side. The Handler sees which of the 25 words are agents. The Handler gives a one-word clue and a number. The Agent uses the clue to find the agents.

Find all 15 agents to win. A bystander ends the turn. A trap ends the game.

Two ways to play

  • Pass one iPhone between two players. This works with no internet connection.
  • Play on two iPhones. One player makes a room and gives the six-letter code to the other player.
