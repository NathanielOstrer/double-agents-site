Privacy Policy

Double AgentsSupportPrivacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Double Agents has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Play on one iPhone

When you play on one iPhone, the app sends nothing. The game stays on your iPhone.

Play on two iPhones

When you make or join an online room, the app signs you in to Google Firebase without an account. Firebase gives your app an anonymous user ID. The app does not know who you are.

The app then sends these items to Firebase servers, so the other player's iPhone can follow the game:

  • The player name that you type
  • The anonymous user ID
  • The game moves: the cards, the clues, the guesses and the score

Anyone who has the room code can see the player names and the game in that room. When the last player leaves, the app deletes the room and its data.

Google Firebase processes this data for the app. Read the Firebase privacy information.

Data on your iPhone

The app saves your player name and your settings on your iPhone: the timer, the turn limit and the sound setting. Delete the app to remove them.

Children

The app does not ask for an age, an email address or any contact details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
