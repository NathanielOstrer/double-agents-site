To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue on GitHub. Tell us your iPhone model and your iOS version.

Common questions

How do we play on two iPhones?
On the first iPhone, select Create Online Game, enter your name and tap Create Room. Give the six-letter room code to the other player. On the second iPhone, select Join Online Game, enter your name and the code, and tap Join Room. The player who made the room starts the game and is the Handler.

The app says that there is no room with this code, or that the room has expired.
Check the code. A room accepts new players for 60 minutes after it is made. After that, make a new room.

The other player left the game.
When the last player leaves, the room is deleted. Make a new room to play again.

I cannot hear the sounds.
Tap the speaker button on the game board to turn the sounds on. The sounds follow the silent switch on your iPhone.

Does the app need an account?
No. See the Privacy Policy.
